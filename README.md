# LaoLao ລາວລາວ: Lao Language Learning Platform

**LaoLao** is a web platform for students learning the Lao language (ພາສາລາວ), built from authentic sentence patterns, the 27 Lao consonants (ອັກສອນລາວ), vowels, tone rules, native audio, interactive handwriting studio, and structured curriculum.

- **Learner App** (`/`):
  - **Sentence Patterns & Generator**: Real conversational Lao sentence patterns with slot-filling generator, romanization, audio pronunciation, and English & Lao translations.
  - **Lao Alphabet & Phonetics** (`/pinyin` / `nav_pinyin`): All 27 Lao consonants organized by class (Middle, High, Low) with traditional mnemonic names (ກ ໄກ່, ຂ ໄຂ່, ຄ ຄວາຍ...), vowels with short/long pairs, and 6 Vientiane Lao tones.
  - **Script & Handwriting Studio** (`/chars` / `nav_chars`): Interactive canvas for practicing Lao calligraphy and letter stroke writing with guided loops ("ຂຽນຫົວ ກ່ອນ").
  - **Lao Dictionary** (`/dict`): Instant search by Lao script, phonetic romanization (e.g. `sabaidee`), or English.
  - **Practice & Quizzes**: Word order, fill in the blank, listen and choose, translation, Lao tone quiz, and handwriting practice.
  - **Spaced Repetition Review (SRS)**: Automatic flashcard review for learned Lao patterns and vocabulary.
  - **Multilingual Interface**: English, Lao (ພາສາລາວ), and Chinese.

- **Admin Portal** (`/admin/`):
  - CMS for Lao lessons, sentence patterns, grammar guides, vocabulary, dialogues, and quizzes.
  - Learner management and subscriptions.
  - One-click bundle publishing.

- **Offline Ready**: Full PWA support with service worker and local browser storage.
