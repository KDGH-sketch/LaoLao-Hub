Every lesson is tied to something real you will see, hear or join in Laos. Tap Listen to hear the key word.

Tak Bat
ຕັກບາດ

Dawn almsgiving in Luang Prabang. Learn to say ໃສ່ບາດ (sai bat), to offer alms.

Baci
ບາສີ

Cotton strings tied on the wrist to call the soul back. The act is ຜູກແຂນ (phuk khen).

Khene
ແຄນ

The bamboo mouth organ behind mor lam music. Say ແຄນ (khaen) and feel the breath.

Boat racing festival
ແຂ່ງເຮືອ

Long boats race the Mekong after the end of Buddhist Lent. ແຂ່ງເຮືອ (khaeng heua).
