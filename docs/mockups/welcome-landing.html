Festival calendar

The Lao year, month by month

Lunar festival dates move each year. The current one is highlighted.

  • 13–16 April

    Boun Pi Mai Lao

    ບຸນປີໃໝ່ລາວ

    Lao New Year. Neighbors splash water to wash away the old year, and Nang Sangkhan parades through Luang Prabang.

  • May

    Boun Bang Fai

    ບຸນບັ້ງໄຟ

    The rocket festival. Villages launch bamboo rockets to call the rains before rice planting.

  • July, full moon

    Boun Khao Phansa

    ບຸນເຂົ້າພັນສາ

    The start of Buddhist Lent. Families bring candles and offerings to the temple.

  • October

    Boun Ok Phansa and Suang Heua

    ບຸນອອກພັນສາ · ບຸນສ່ວງເຮືອ

    End of Lent. Lit boats float on the Mekong the night before, and crews race long boats the next day.

  • November, full moon

    Boun That Luang

    ບຸນທາດຫຼວງ

    Candlelit processions and a fair at Pha That Luang in Vientiane, for up to a week.

  • 2 December

    Lao National Day

    ວັນຊາດ

    Marks the founding of the Lao People's Democratic Republic in 1975.
